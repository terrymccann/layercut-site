> License keys start with LayerCut 1.0. During the beta, every feature is unlocked without one.

## Where to find your key

After you buy Pro, your license key is:

- shown on screen right after checkout, with a button to copy it or save it as a file
- emailed to the address you bought with, from `licenses@layercut.app`, with the license file attached

## Entering your key

1. Open LayerCut.
2. Choose **LayerCut → License…** on a Mac, or **Help → License…** on Windows.
3. Paste the key, or click **Load license file…** and choose the attached file. You can also drag the license file onto the LayerCut window.

Pro features unlock straight away. **About LayerCut** shows who the license belongs to and when its updates end.

## A new computer

Enter the same key on the new computer. There's nothing to deactivate on the old one, since a license is for one person on any computers they use.

## Lost your key?

Email [hello@layercut.app](mailto:hello@layercut.app) from the address you bought with, or include your Paddle order number, and we'll send it again.

## Renewing updates

Your license includes updates for 12 months. When they end, LayerCut tells you when a newer version is available. Renewing costs $25 and adds 12 months from the day your current updates end, or from today if they've already ended. You'll get a new key by email.
