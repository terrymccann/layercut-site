**If LayerCut Pro doesn't work for you, ask for a refund within 30 days of purchase and you'll get your money back.** No questions asked.

## How to ask

Reply to your receipt email from Paddle, or contact [Paddle's buyer support](https://paddle.net/). You can also email [hello@layercut.app](mailto:hello@layercut.app) with your order number or the email address you bought with.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks usually take 5–10 working days to show them.

## After a refund

Your license key stops working in versions released after the refund, and you can keep using the free edition.

## Renewals

Update renewals can also be refunded within 30 days.

## Try before you buy

The free edition shows every Pro feature in the 3D and Sheets views, so you can check that your models work before buying.
