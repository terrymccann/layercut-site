## Now: getting to version 1.0 {: .rm-now }

The work that turns the beta into a finished product.

- **Signed installers for Mac and Windows.** Notarized by Apple and signed for Windows, so LayerCut opens without security warnings.
- **Automatic updates.** LayerCut tells you when a new version is out and installs it when you say so.
- **Pro licenses.** Buy Pro on this site and unlock it with a license key, with no account needed.
- **Windows polish.** Ctrl shortcuts throughout, “Show in Explorer” after export, and other Windows conventions.

</section>

<section class="rm-stage" markdown="1">

## Next {: .rm-next }

Improvements to the build modes and the cut sheets.

- **Living hinge settings.** Choose the slit length, gap and spacing for your material, with a small bend test piece to cut first.
- **Grid slices at any angle.** Interlocking slices that cross at angles other than 90°, to follow a model's shape.
- **Pick the radial center.** Set the center of a radial build yourself, for models that aren't centered on their footprint.
- **Common-line cutting.** Where two pieces share a straight edge, cut it once. Saves cutting time and material.
- **Finer rotation when nesting.** Turn pieces by less than 90° to fit more on each sheet.
- **More checks before you cut.** Warnings for features too thin to survive in stacked layers.

</section>

<section class="rm-stage" markdown="1">

## Later {: .rm-later }

Bigger pieces of work we plan to take on after that.

- **Curve-following slices.** Interlocking slices that bend to follow a model's surface, for more organic shapes.
- **More file formats.** Open 3MF and STEP models, and export DXF and PDF cut files.
- **LayerCut in the browser.** Try LayerCut without installing anything.

</section>
